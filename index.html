Featured research
Caltech · Thomson Lab · with Shichen Liu & Matt Thomson

Lineage-anchored optimal transport

Existing methods for tracking cell trajectories through time only use lineage-barcode data to check their answers after the fact — never to guide the transport solution itself. LA-OT pre-assigns transport mass to a cell's known clonal relatives before solving, which resolves a tradeoff where common cell types and rare ones can't both be predicted well by the same model.

+21% cosine similarity +33% top-1 accuracy pDC rescue: 0.253 → 0.894
